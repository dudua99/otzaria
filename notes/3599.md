Type: BUG follow-up, approved by a maintainer. Base: upstream/dev 4fe1973a4.

## What is still open
The first part (panel marker letter shown even when body markers are hidden) was fixed and merged in #5580 (7c8329471, 98353ccec).
The reporter's later screenshot (y0534143369-del) shows, inside the group "רש"י על שופטים", item titles "רש"י על שופטים ו, ה" and "רש"י על שופטים ו, ח".
Two different source lines, so the titles are kept on purpose (#896, 443d1e63a). Y-PLONI wrote the next step in his diagnosis:
"לקצר בכותרות הללו את שם הספר שכבר מופיע בכותרת הקבוצה". So show "ו, ה" / "ו, ח" under the group header "רש"י על שופטים".

## Evidence (dev)
- Group header = `group.bookTitle`: lib/text_book/view/commentary_list_base.dart:1711-1714.
- Item title = `link.displayReference` (book title + ref; lib/models/links.dart:353-363). It is rendered in lib/text_book/view/commentary_list_items.dart:244-299 (`displayTitle`, `showTitle`, `markerText`).
- The decision is in `_shouldShowItemTitle`, commentary_list_base.dart:1737-1752. Token helpers are in lib/text_book/utils/commentary_title_visibility.dart (`referenceTokens`, `_isTokenPrefix`; they already tolerate nikud and gershayim).

## Fix idea
Add a pure helper in commentary_title_visibility.dart: `String commentaryTitleWithoutBook({displayTitle, groupBookTitle})`.
It returns the location part when the token prefix of `displayTitle` equals the tokens of `groupBookTitle` and the remainder is non-empty. Otherwise it returns `displayTitle` unchanged (if unsure, keep the full title).
Cut on the ORIGINAL string, not the tokens: walk the characters while you match the tokens, then trim the leading separators. Apply it in commentary_list_items.dart only when `showTitle` is true, before the marker letter is prepended and before replaceHolyNames.
Pass the group title through a new optional parameter, or reuse the existing `shouldShowItemTitle` pattern with a `shortenItemTitle` callback.

## Pitfalls
- Copy: `onLinkTitleRendered` feeds multi-item copy (commentary_list_base `_renderedTitleByKey`). Decide whether the copied text should keep the full ref. Default: report the FULL title for copy, so copied text stays self-contained, and say so in BODY.
- Range titles (`_rangeDisplaySuffix`) and titles that are only the book name: the remainder is empty, so keep the full title.
- The same widget is used in the links/commentary panels of the page-shape and combined views. Check that every caller has a group header. If a caller has no header (flat list), do NOT shorten.
- Keep `repeatsPrevious` working on the full title.
