## Requested behavior
Reporter: reading with the keyboard, the arrow keys are clumsy — "it takes time until it responds, so a long press moves too much and a short press moves a little". Asks (1) faster/more controllable arrow scrolling, (2) PageUp/PageDown to scroll one screen.

(2) is ALREADY DONE: 0b07a7b88 (26.10, issue #3272 "PageDown/PageUp גוללים ומדפדפים בקוראים") — `animatePageScroll` = 80% of the viewport, in the continuous reader (progressive_scrolling.dart L14-28, `_handlePageKey` L322-338), page-shape (simple_text_viewer.dart L276-287, L1899-1909) and PDF. It is in 0.9.98.3 (ancestor of tag 0.9.98.3+809); the reporter is on 0.9.98.0. Only verify it still works; say so in BODY.

(1) DEFAULT (no new setting):
- Response is immediate: KeyDown of ↓/↑ (no modifiers) moves one step right away (the existing 100px tap step, today applied only on KeyUp, L284-312), then, if the key is still held, continuous scrolling starts.
- Hold = controllable speed: start ≈ 600 px/s, gentle exponential ramp (curve ≈ 1.0), cap ≈ 2500 px/s (≈ 3 screens/s), reached after ≈ 1.4 s. Today the reader uses accelerationFactor 312.5, curve 10, maxSpeed 10000 → full 10000 px/s after 0.35 s (≈ 7500 px in a 1 s hold) — that is the "a long press goes too far".
- KeyUp after a hold: no extra 100px jump (the step was already given on KeyDown); a quick tap = exactly one step.
- Exact numbers are a tuning decision — keep them in ONE named const set (e.g. `ReaderArrowScrollTuning`) used by all four call sites, and state the chosen values + the measured distances in BODY.
Acceptance:
- Tap ↓ once in the continuous reader → content moves ≈ 100px (one step), visible on the next frame (fails on dev: nothing moves until KeyUp, then +100px animation).
- Hold ↓ 1 s → moved between ~800 and ~2000 px (fails on dev: ~7500 px).
- Hold ↓ 3 s → speed capped (≤ cap × time + step).
- PageDown/PageUp → 80% viewport (unchanged; regression test exists or add one).
- Same tuning in the commentary list and the PDF commentary panel (same three numbers today).
