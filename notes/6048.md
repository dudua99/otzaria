# 6048 — Shift+Down after a one-line mouse selection drops the selection

## Summary
Select text with the mouse within ONE line, then Shift+ArrowDown: the existing selection disappears instead of being extended. When the mouse selection spans more than one line, Shift+Down extends correctly.

## Evidence / where to look
- `lib/widgets/text/rtl_selection_shortcuts.dart`: custom physical-arrow extend actions; `_selectionGeometry` (~351) distinguishes single-line vs multi-line selections (`multiLine`, `reversed`) and the drag-direction tracking ("מעקב כיוון גרירת הבחירה"). Vertical Shift+Up/Down is not handled there explicitly — check whether it falls to Flutter's `ExtendSelectionVerticallyToAdjacentLineIntent` or is intercepted.
- `lib/shortcuts/keyboard_shortcuts.dart:133-172` `_handleScopedKeyEvent` / `_focusArrows` (plain arrows only) and 42da7c3bc (18.11, routing released arrows by `hasFocusedSelectableRegionSelection`).
- Check whether the reader's SelectionArea per line (each line its own selectable) loses the base when the extent moves to the next selectable while base/extent are on the same line (the single-line case).

## Reproduce first
Widget test: reader with 3+ lines in SelectionArea, drag-select inside line 1 (one line), send Shift+ArrowDown -> expected selection still starts at the original base and grows; dev: empty/moved. Also the multi-line control case. If the drop happens in pure Flutter `SelectableRegion` without any app action involved, mark NOT READY with evidence (framework bug) — unless an app-level minimal workaround is obvious.

## Pitfalls
- RTL: Flutter swaps left/right; vertical should not be swapped.
- Do not break #5925/#5904 selection-keeping fixes (8f04eef88, 5b9b3fc3f) or test/shortcuts/reader_arrow_routing_test.dart.
