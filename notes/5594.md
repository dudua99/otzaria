Acceptance (to be confirmed by the reproduction):

## Hypotheses to check (in this order)
1. Estimated extent drift: positioned_sliver_list's maxScrollExtent is an estimate that changes as unseen items are laid out. Because 11259f78c moves content RELATIVELY (pixels + delta) while the thumb is frozen at `_thumbPosition`, the two drift apart; when the drag ends `_updateMetrics` snaps the thumb (visible jump), and `range` used in `_onDragUpdate` changes between frames (pixel delta per thumb px changes → "goes down too much").
2. Scale only: in a 10k-line book range ≈ 1-2M px, track ≈ 800px → 1 thumb px ≈ 2-3 screens. Then the behavior is proportional and the "jump" is inherent (every scrollbar does this on a huge document); plus each frame lays out a brand-new screen (jank). Measure the actual px-per-thumb-px on a real long book.
